Allowed sender mailboxes

The only mailboxes host rules and test messages can send as. One address per line. Each one must also be in the app's Exchange scope, otherwise Graph refuses to send. Set custom attribute 15 to GraphRelay on the mailbox, either way:

  • Exchange admin center (admin.exchange.microsoft.com): Recipients → Mailboxes → click the mailbox → Others → Custom attributes → field 15 = GraphRelay → Save.
  • PowerShell: Set-Mailbox <address> -CustomAttribute15 "GraphRelay"

Exchange can take 30 minutes to 2 hours to apply it.
